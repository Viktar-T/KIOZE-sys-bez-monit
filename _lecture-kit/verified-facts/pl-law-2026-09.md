## Prawo budowlane — consolidated text Dz.U. 2026 poz. 524 (Obwieszczenie Marszałka Sejmu z 27.03.2026, published 16.04.2026)

ISAP entry: https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20260000524 · ELI API: https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/524

| Paragraph | Permit | Notification (zgłoszenie) | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| ust. 1 | no | yes | construction (budowa) |
| ust. 2 | no | no | construction (budowa) |
| ust. 3 | no | yes | construction works, incl. installation (roboty budowlane, instalowanie) |
| ust. 4 | no | no | construction works, incl. installation |

**PV above 6.5 kW — art. 29 ust. 4 pkt 3 lit. c**
- Installing PV up to 150 kW (and energy storage up to 30 kWh) needs neither a permit nor a notification.
- For PV above 6.5 kW three obligations still apply:
  - agreement of the design with a fire-safety expert ("uzgodnienie z rzeczoznawcą do spraw zabezpieczeń przeciwpożarowych");
  - notification of PSP on completion;
  - handing PSP "plan urządzenia fotowoltaicznego dla ekip ratowniczych" together with that notification.

**Art. 56**
- ust. 1 pkt 2: notify PSP if the project required fire-safety agreement.
- ust. 1a: repealed ("uchylony").

## Energy storage — Ustawa z 4.12.2025 o zmianie ustawy – Prawo budowlane oraz niektórych innych ustaw, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1847

- In force 7.01.2026.

  - art. 1 pkt 1 lit. a and c, i.e. the art. 3 definitions, incl. pkt 27 "magazyn energii elektrycznej";
  - art. 1 pkt 3.
- ELI API: https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/1847 · ISAP: https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20250001847

**Thresholds (nominal capacity)**

Free-standing (wolno stojący):
- ≤ 30 kWh: no permit, no notification (art. 29 ust. 2 pkt 39).
- > 30 to ≤ 300 kWh:
  - notification (art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 40);
  - fire-safety agreement of the site plan (art. 33 ust. 3, 1st sentence);
  - notification of PSP with a plan showing the location and data important for rescue teams (art. 33 ust. 3, 2nd sentence).
- > 300 to ≤ 2000 kWh:
  - notification (art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 3c);
  - fire-safety agreement of the site plan AND the architectural-construction design (art. 33 ust. 4bb);
  - notification of PSP with the plan.
- > 2000 kWh: permit (no exemption).

Installed on a building (instalowanie na obiekcie budowlanym):
- ≤ 30 kWh: no permit, no notification (art. 29 ust. 4 pkt 3 lit. c).
- > 30 to ≤ 300 kWh:
  - notification (art. 29 ust. 3 pkt 3 lit. h);
  - technical documentation agreed for fire safety attached to the notification (art. 30, pkt 3c of the list of documents);
  - notification of PSP with the plan (art. 33 ust. 3).
- > 300 kWh: no exemption in art. 29, so a building permit is required.

**PV provisions in the draft of 6.08.2026**
- § 303: DC cables run outside the building. Inside the building they are allowed only:
  - in or under plaster in A1/A2,d0 partitions with ≥ 5 mm of plaster; or
  - in an enclosure of EI 30 (buildings under § 224) or EI 60 (other buildings).
- § 304, "wyłącznik awaryjny PV" for rescue teams. It must either:
  - reduce the DC voltage outside the module zone to < 30 V within ≤ 30 s and keep it there; or
  - disconnect the DC cables inside the building.
- § 305 ust. 3, minimum protective means:
  1. a device monitoring the DC insulation condition;
  2. a DC arc-fault detection and interruption device meeting the Polish Standard on DC arc detection and interruption (i.e. PN-EN IEC 63027).
- § 305 ust. 4: smoke detector in the inverter/switchboard room.
- § 306: roof PV sectors ≤ 1600 m², span ≤ 40 m, 5 m between sectors.

**BESS provisions in the draft of 6.08.2026 (BESS = storage > 2 kWh in secondary cells)**
- § 311: BESS may be installed only with a BMS or other protective means that:
  1. continuously monitor voltage, current and pack temperature;
  2. prevent over-charge and over-discharge;
  3. prevent excessive current;
  4. protect against short circuit;
  5. signal failures and abnormal states, esp. over-temperature;
  6. react by emergency disconnection of the battery.
- § 312: "wyłącznik awaryjny BESS" for rescue teams (except circuits ≤ 12 V AC / 30 V DC).
- § 313:
  - ≤ 600 kWh per fire zone in a building not dedicated to BESS;
  - placement limits by capacity (≤ 60, 60–300, > 300 kWh);
  - not above 25 m.
- § 315, room where gas may be released in normal operation:
  - ventilation that keeps the concentration < 25% LEL on average;
  - an explosive-atmosphere detection device that (a) raises the alarm, (b) controls the ventilation, (c) disconnects the batteries.
- § 316, room > 60 kWh where gas may be released during failure (esp. thermal runaway):
  - gas detection with alarm and disconnection;
  - emergency exhaust ventilation ≥ 10 air changes/h when > 120 kWh;
  - explosion relief ≥ 0.065 m²/m³.
- § 317: ≤ 30 kWh needs an autonomous smoke detector plus a CO detector, and so on.
- NOTE: the 10% LEL alarm / 30% LEL disconnect figures quoted by Kanał Elektryczny (Apr 2026) are NOT in the 6.08.2026 draft.
