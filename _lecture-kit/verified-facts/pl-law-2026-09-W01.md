## KSC amendment — Ustawa z 23.01.2026 o zmianie ustawy o krajowym systemie cyberbezpieczeństwa oraz niektórych innych ustaw, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 252

ELI: https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/252 · ISAP: https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20260000252 — status "obowiązujący", entry into force 2026-04-03 (art. 49: one month after promulgation). Text read in the PDF.

- Art. 5 ust. 1 pkt 1 KSC (new wording): **podmiot kluczowy** = an entity listed in annex 1 that exceeds the thresholds for a medium-sized enterprise (art. 2 ust. 1 annex I to Reg. 651/2014). Art. 5 ust. 2 pkt 1: **podmiot ważny** = an annex 1 entity that meets the medium-enterprise thresholds and is not a key entity.
- Annex 1, sector "Energia", subsector "Energia elektryczna" lists, among others: przedsiębiorstwo energetyczne (Prawo energetyczne art. 3 pkt 12) **holding a concession for electricity generation** ("posiadające koncesję na wykonywanie działalności gospodarczej w zakresie wytwarzania energii elektrycznej"); transmission, distribution and trading licensees; market participants providing services under Prawo energetyczne art. 3 pkt 6e, 11j, 59, 59a; entities under art. 3 pkt 28b; charging-point operators.
- Art. 33 ust. 1 (transitional): entities that meet the criteria on the day of entry into force fulfil the obligations of chapter 3 of the KSC (incl. the information security management system) **within 12 months** of entry into force (→ 3.04.2027).
- Art. 33 ust. 2: key entities carry out the **first audit** (art. 15 ust. 1) **within 24 months** (→ 3.04.2028).
- Art. 33 ust. 3 and art. 34 ust. 3 pkt 1: the application for entry into the "wykaz podmiotów kluczowych i podmiotów ważnych" is filed according to a **schedule announced by the minister for digitisation** in his official journal (the act itself does not fix 3.10.2026; the date comes from the schedule — gov.pl pages quote self-registration until 3.10.2026, see `research/W01/pl_data_update.md`).
- Art. 34 ust. 1: the minister for digitisation creates the wykaz within a month of entry into force.
- Art. 35: fines under art. 73 ust. 1–4, 73a–73c, 76b may first be imposed **2 years** after entry into force.

## Fire protection act

- Ustawa z 24.08.1991 o ochronie przeciwpożarowej, consolidated text **Dz.U. 2025 poz. 188** (Obwieszczenie z 5.02.2025), status "obowiązujący". ISAP: https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20250000188
  - **Art. 4 ust. 1**: the owner of a building, structure or site, ensuring its fire protection, must: (1) comply with fire-safety technical, installation and technological requirements; (2) equip it with the required fire-protection devices and extinguishers; (3) ensure inspections, maintenance and repairs of fire-protection devices "w sposób gwarantujący ich sprawne i niezawodne funkcjonowanie"; (4) ensure safety and evacuation of persons; (5) prepare the building/site for a rescue operation; (6) acquaint employees with fire regulations; (7) set out procedures in case of fire or other local hazard.
  - Art. 4 ust. 1a: responsibility may pass to the manager or user under a civil-law contract; otherwise it rests on the person actually in control of the building or site.

## EU law read in EUR-Lex (29.09.2026)

**Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542** — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023R1542
- Art. 12 "Safety of stationary battery energy storage systems": (1) stationary BESS placed on the market or put into service shall be safe during normal operation and use; (2) **by 18 August 2024** the technical documentation (Annex VIII) shall (a) demonstrate compliance and include evidence of successful testing for the **safety parameters in Annex V** (state-of-the-art methods; parameters apply in so far as the hazard exists), (b) assess hazards not addressed in Annex V, (c) include evidence that those hazards were mitigated and tested, (d) include **mitigation instructions** in case the hazards occur, "for example a fire or explosion".
- Annex V "Safety parameters" (11 items): 1 thermal shock and cycling; 2 external short circuit protection; 3 overcharge protection; 4 over-discharge protection; 5 over-temperature protection; 6 **thermal propagation protection**; 7 mechanical damage by external forces; 8 internal short circuit; 9 thermal abuse; 10 fire test; 11 **emission of gases**.
- Art. 13(1): label with general information (Annex VI Part A) from **18 August 2026 or 18 months after the implementing act, whichever is later**; Annex VI Part A includes, among others, the chemistry, hazardous substances and "usable extinguishing agent".
- Art. 77(1): **battery passport from 18 February 2027** for LMT batteries, industrial batteries > 2 kWh and EV batteries.
- Art. 96: applies from 18.02.2024; Chapter VI (conformity assessment) and Art. 17 from 18.08.2024; Chapter VIII from 18.08.2025; Art. 11 from 18.02.2027.
- **Regulation (EU) 2025/1561** of 18.07.2025 (OJ 30.7.2025), Art. 1: in Art. 48(1) of 2023/1542 the date 18.08.2025 is replaced by **18.08.2027** (due diligence policies); in Art. 48(5) 18.02.2025 → 26.07.2026. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32025R1561

**Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/339** of 16.02.2026 (OJ L 29.4.2026), Art. 1: "Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 is repealed with effect from **11 December 2027**" (RED cybersecurity requirements; the CRA applies fully from 11.12.2027). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32026R0339

**Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230** — the original OJ text of Art. 54 reads "14 January 2027"; EUR-Lex document information (dates of effect, after corrigenda 32023R1230R(01)–(03)) gives **application 20/01/2027**, entry into force 19/07/2023, partial application 20/01/2024 (Arts 26–42), 20/07/2024, 20/10/2026.
