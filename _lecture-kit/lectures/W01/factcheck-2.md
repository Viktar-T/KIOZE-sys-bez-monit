### Verified OK

- **ISO 12100:2010:** its narrower harm definition, residual risk (3.13) and the three-step method all check out. It replaced ISO 12100-1:2003. The step names are fine, including „informacje dotyczące użytkowania”.
- **Görnemann 2019:** "shall be applied in the following sequence".
- **CIOP-PIB:** Polish wording from PN-EN ISO 12100-1:2005 pkt 3.6.
